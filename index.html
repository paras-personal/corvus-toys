Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of toys does CorVus Toys sell?

We curate a focused range of soft plush toys, toddler ride-ons like balance bikes, remote control cars, pop it fidget toys, and collectible character keychains and charms.

How do I check if a toy is in stock?

Use the contact form below with the toy name, and we will get back to you with availability, pricing, and gifting suggestions.

Are these toys good for return gifts and party favours?

Yes! Pop it fidget toys, keychains, and charms are popular return gifts, and we can suggest bundles for birthdays and festive gifting.

What ages are these toys suitable for?

Every product page lists an age guide. Soft toys start at 3+, ride-ons suit toddlers from 1 to 3 years with supervision, and collectibles suit ages 5 and up.
